Christmas tech gift ideas by age

Not sure what to get? Start with their age, then filter by budget. Every link below opens our gadget finder pre-filtered for you.

  2. Teens (13–17)

Kids (5–12)

Think screen-free audio players, instant cameras, volume-limited headphones and family gaming. Set up parental controls before the big day. See gifts for kids →

Teens (13–17)

Earbuds, keychain and instant cameras, LED room lights, gaming accessories and creator kit dominate teen wish lists. See gifts for teens →

Adults (18–54)

Noise-cancelling headphones, smart rings, kitchen gadgets, desk upgrades and handheld gaming are popular picks. Open the finder for adults →

Grandparents and parents (55+)

Digital photo frames, smart displays for video calls, e-readers with adjustable text and smartwatches with safety features. Set them up first. See gifts for grandparents →

Timing and returns

  • Black Friday (27 November 2026) and Cyber Monday (30 November) are the main discount days. See the deals calendar.
  • Check each retailer’s last order dates for Christmas delivery; they vary by store and country.
  • Ask for a gift receipt and check holiday return windows.
