Switch 2 vs Steam Deck vs Xbox Ally vs PlayStation Portal

Handheld gaming has never had more choice. They look similar, but they play very different games. Start with what you want to play.

By the ViralGadgets editorial team · 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Branden Skeli on Unsplash

At a glance

HandheldPlaysBest for
Nintendo Switch 2Nintendo games, most Switch 1 gamesFamilies, Nintendo fans, TV + handheld
Steam Deck OLEDYour Steam (PC) libraryPC gamers on a budget
ROG Xbox Ally / Ally XPC games: Xbox PC, Game Pass, Steam and moreGame Pass and Windows gamers
PlayStation PortalStreams games from your PS5 (and cloud streaming where offered)PS5 owners who share the TV

The questions that matter

  • Where are your games? Your existing library usually decides it.
  • Do you need it to work offline? The PlayStation Portal needs a PS5 and strong Wi-Fi.
  • Battery: all of them drain fast in demanding games; reviewers flagged Switch 2 and PC handheld battery life in particular.
  • Budget: the ROG Xbox Ally X is the priciest of the group.

Volt’s quick picks

For most families: Switch 2. For PC gamers: Steam Deck OLED. For Game Pass fans with budget: ROG Xbox Ally X. For PS5 owners: PlayStation Portal. Browse them all in the gaming finder.
