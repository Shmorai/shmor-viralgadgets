Smart bird feeders: how Bird Buddy and friends went viral

A bird feeder with a camera that sends “postcards” of every visitor turned garden birds into a TikTok genre. Here’s what you get, and what costs extra.

By the ViralGadgets editorial team · 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Andrey Larionov on Unsplash

How they work

A camera built into the feeder wakes when a bird lands, records photos or short clips, and sends them to your phone. AI in the app suggests which species it is. Many models have solar roofs to keep the battery topped up.

Free vs Premium (Bird Buddy)

Bird Buddy’s support pages say the feeder is fully functional without a membership: you get visitor photos and clips, live view and AI identification. Its Premium plan adds extras such as the highest video quality, more AI features, naming repeat visitors and more storage. Reviewers disagree on whether Premium is worth it; it suits keen birders who use the app a lot.

Before you buy

  • Wi-Fi reach: the feeder needs a solid signal where you mount it.
  • Placement: good light, away from squirrels if you can, and somewhere you can reach to refill.
  • Patience: it can take days or weeks for birds to find a new feeder.

It’s a lovely gift for grandparents and nature-loving kids. See it in our gifts for grandparents.
