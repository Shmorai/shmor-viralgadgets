How to choose wireless earbuds in five questions

Earbuds are one of the most-gifted gadgets every year. Answer these five questions and the right pair gets much easier to spot.

By the ViralGadgets editorial team · 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: TheRegisti on Unsplash

On this page

  1. 1. Which phone do you use?
  2. 2. Do you need noise cancelling?
  3. 3. How do they fit?
  4. 4. Battery and case
  5. 5. Sweat and water

1. Which phone do you use?

AirPods work best with iPhone (instant pairing, spatial audio, Find My). Samsung’s Galaxy Buds get extra features with Galaxy phones. Brands like Sony, Bose, Nothing and JBL work well across both.

2. Do you need noise cancelling?

For commutes, flights and open-plan offices, active noise cancellation (ANC) is worth it. If you run near traffic, look for a good transparency mode, or consider open-ear headphones that don’t block your ears at all.

3. How do they fit?

Fit affects sound, noise cancelling and comfort more than any spec. Stem-style buds suit some ears; rounder buds with silicone tips suit others. Buy from a store with an easy return policy.

4. Battery and case

Look at listening time per charge (with ANC on) and total time with the case. Wireless charging is a nice extra, not a must.

5. Sweat and water

Check the IP rating if you train hard or run in the rain. IPX4 handles sweat and splashes; higher numbers handle more.
