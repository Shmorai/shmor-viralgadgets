Where to spend first

  1. Sound: a clip-on wireless mic is the biggest upgrade for talking-to-camera videos.
  2. Stability: a tripod or gimbal makes footage instantly look more professional.
  3. Light: face a window, or add a small LED light.
  4. Camera: only then think about a dedicated camera; modern phones are very capable.
