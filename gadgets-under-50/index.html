Getting the most from a small budget

  • Buy the accessory, not the device. A magnetic power bank or a selfie tripod upgrades a phone they already love.
  • Watch the hidden extras. Instant cameras need film, trackers need a phone of the right brand.
  • Cheap chargers and power banks need care: stick to known brands with safety marks; badly made batteries can overheat.
  • Sales help most here. Small gadgets often get the biggest percentage discounts around Black Friday.
