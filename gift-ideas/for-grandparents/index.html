Make it easy from day one

  • Set it up before you wrap it: connect it to Wi-Fi, create accounts and load photos or books.
  • Pick big, clear screens and adjustable text sizes (e-readers and smart displays do this well).
  • Safety features help: Apple Watch offers fall detection and emergency SOS; video doorbells let people see who’s calling first.
  • Check subscriptions so they aren’t surprised by a bill later.
