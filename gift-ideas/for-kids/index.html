Tips for buying tech for younger children

  • Protect their hearing: choose headphones with a volume limit (often 85 dB).
  • Screen-free counts: audio players like Yoto and Tonies, and instant cameras, are great alternatives to another screen.
  • Set up parental controls first on consoles, tablets and smart speakers, before the box is opened.
  • Watch for small parts and button batteries with younger children: keep coin batteries and trackers out of reach.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good first “phone” alternative for a child?
Kids’ smartwatches such as Garmin Bounce allow calls and messages with approved contacts plus location sharing, without full smartphone access. They usually need a monthly data plan, so check costs and availability in your country.
