Tips for buying tech for teenagers

  • Ask about their phone before buying earbuds, trackers or controllers: compatibility matters.
  • Creator kit is a safe bet for teens who film: a clip-on mic or tripod is cheaper than a camera and makes a bigger difference.
  • Check age ratings and account rules for consoles, VR headsets and games, and set up family controls together.
  • Room-makeover gadgets (LED strips, sunset lamps) are cheap, fun and endlessly TikTok-able.
