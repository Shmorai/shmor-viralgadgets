Before you buy a kitchen gadget

  • Measure your counter and cupboards. Dual-basket air fryers and ice cream makers are bulky.
  • Check the plug and voltage if buying from another country.
  • Look at what’s dishwasher-safe: it decides whether you’ll actually use it.
