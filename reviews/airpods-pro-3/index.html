AirPods Pro 3: Volt’s verdict

Apple’s top earbuds added stronger noise cancelling and heart-rate sensing. Most reviewers call them Apple’s best yet, but fit divides opinion.

A research-based rating: the ViralGadgets team compares published reviews, long-term tests and specs. We haven't tested it hands-on. How Volt rates

What reviewers love

  • Stronger noise cancelling, with an excellent transparency mode
  • Sound most reviewers prefer to the Pro 2
  • Heart-rate sensing that reviewers found reliable for workouts
  • More ear-tip sizes, including XXS

What gives them pause

  • Fit is divisive: some testers found it less secure than the Pro 2
  • No conventional equaliser
  • Many features are iPhone-only
  • Premium price, and no charging cable in the box

Best for: iPhone owners who want the best noise cancelling in a small earbud.

Skip it if: You use Android, or your AirPods Pro 2 already fit perfectly.

Why everyone is talking about it

AirPods Pro are a fixture of “what’s in my bag” videos, and the third generation added workout heart-rate tracking, which made them a fitness-creator favourite too.

What the reviews agree on

Tom’s Guide called them the best AirPods yet, praising the noise cancelling and transparency mode. What Hi-Fi called them delightfully dependable, especially for iOS users, and Digital Trends liked them after months of use. The dissent is about fit: T3 found the redesigned shape less secure than the Pro 2, which hurt real-world noise cancelling, while RTINGS and Tom’s Guide found the fit comfortable and stable.

Volt’s take

On an iPhone, these are an easy recommendation, but buy from a store with simple returns so you can check the fit. Already happy with your Pro 2? The upgrade is nice, not necessary. Compare with the cheaper AirPods 5, which now have noise cancelling too.
