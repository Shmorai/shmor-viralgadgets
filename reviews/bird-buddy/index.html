A research-based rating: the ViralGadgets team compares published reviews, long-term tests and specs. We haven't tested it hands-on. How Volt rates

What reviewers love

  • Automatic photo and video “postcards” of visiting birds
  • AI species identification in a fun, polished app
  • Works without a subscription for the core features
  • A brilliant gift for families and grandparents

What gives them pause

  • Premium price for a bird feeder
  • Best video quality and extra AI features need Premium
  • Needs solid Wi-Fi where you mount it
  • Some quirks reported in long-term use

Best for: Nature-loving families and grandparents.

Skip it if: Your garden has weak Wi-Fi, or you’d resent paying for Premium extras.

What the reviews agree on

TechRadar praised the Pro model’s joyful app, and its six-month test of the Bird Buddy 2 found the app delightful but the price and small quirks held it back. Bird Buddy’s own support pages confirm core features work without a membership, with Premium adding extras like top video quality and more AI tools.

Volt’s take

One of the most delightful gadgets around, especially as a gift. Start without Premium and upgrade only if you find yourself living in the app. More in our smart bird feeder guide.
