Fujifilm X100VI: Volt’s verdict

TikTok’s favourite camera is genuinely excellent according to reviewers. The hard part is buying one at a fair price.

Photo: Charlie Firth on Unsplash

A brilliant everyday camera. Just don’t pay a scalper for one.

Rated by Volt, our (fictional) robot mascot · 14 October 2026
A research-based rating: the ViralGadgets team compares published reviews, long-term tests and specs. We haven't tested it hands-on. How Volt rates

What reviewers love

  • 40MP sensor with in-body stabilisation
  • Fujifilm’s much-loved film simulation colours
  • Classic, compact design that’s a joy to carry
  • Sharp 35mm-equivalent f/2 lens

What gives them pause

  • Hard to find, with long waiting lists at times
  • Battery life is modest
  • Fixed lens: no zoom
  • Single, slower UHS-I card slot for big files

Best for: Enthusiasts who want one beautiful camera for everyday and travel photos.

Skip it if: You need zoom or video features, or you’d have to pay over the odds to get one.

Why everyone is talking about it

Its predecessor went viral on TikTok for film-like photos straight out of the camera, and the X100VI inherited that hype, with back orders reported by TechRadar.

What the reviews agree on

DPReview scored it 87%, praising the stabilised 40MP sensor. TechRadar called it an everyday delight. Tom’s Guide liked the images but flagged the single UHS-I card slot. Battery life and the fixed lens are the recurring caveats.

Volt’s take

The hype is deserved, the value depends on what you pay. Buy from authorised retailers or join official waiting lists. Alternatives are in our X100VI explainer.
