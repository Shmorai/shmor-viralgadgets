Kindle Paperwhite: Volt’s verdict

BookTok’s favourite gadget got a bigger, faster screen in its latest version. Reviewers love it, but call it an evolution, not a revolution.

What reviewers love

  • Larger 7-inch screen with sharper contrast
  • Noticeably faster page turns than older models
  • Waterproof for bath and beach reading
  • Battery measured in weeks, not days
  • Adjustable text size suits readers of every age

What gives them pause

  • A modest upgrade if you own a recent Paperwhite
  • No physical page-turn buttons
  • Lock-screen ads unless you pay extra to remove them
  • A little pricier than the model it replaced

Best for: First-time e-reader buyers, BookTok fans, and older readers who want bigger text.

Skip it if: You already own a Paperwhite from the last few years, or you want page-turn buttons.

Why everyone is talking about it

BookTok turned reading into one of TikTok’s biggest communities, and the Kindle Paperwhite is the gadget you see in half of those videos. It’s also one of the most heavily discounted devices in Amazon sales, which keeps it on gift lists every year.

What the reviews agree on

Tom’s Guide said the bigger display, faster performance and longer battery justify the price increase. TechRadar praised the improved screen contrast and battery but found the design plain for the price. Expert Reviews called it too familiar to be a must-upgrade, but still the go-to touchscreen e-reader. The repeated catches: no page-turn buttons, and ads on the lock screen unless you pay to remove them.

Volt’s take

For anyone without an e-reader, this is an easy recommendation and a lovely gift. If you already have a Paperwhite from the last few years, keep it. Wait for a sale: Kindles are among the biggest discounts on Prime Day and Black Friday.
