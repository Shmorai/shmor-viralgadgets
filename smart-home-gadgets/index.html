Smart home privacy basics

  • Use a strong, unique password and turn on two-step verification for each smart home app.
  • Review voice-recording and camera settings; most speakers have a mic-off button and displays a camera shutter.
  • Keep devices updated, and buy from brands that promise security updates.
  • Point cameras at your own property. In the UK, the ICO has guidance for home CCTV that captures public areas or neighbours.
