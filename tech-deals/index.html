Deal timing

The tech deals calendar

We don’t post fake “deals”. Instead, here’s when gadget prices genuinely tend to drop, and how to check a deal is real before you buy.

Photo: Samsung Memory on Unsplash

Big sale dates

Dates for the current season. Always confirm on the retailer’s own site.

27 Nov 2026

Black Friday

The biggest tech sale of the year in the US, UK and Europe. Many stores start “Black Friday” offers in early November.

30 Nov 2026

Cyber Monday

Online-focused deals the Monday after Black Friday; some stores run a full “Cyber Week”.

11 Nov

Singles’ Day

A huge shopping day in China that AliExpress and some Western retailers also join.

26 Dec

Boxing Day (UK)

Post-Christmas sales in the UK and some other countries, often running into January.

Usually July

Amazon Prime Day

Amazon’s summer members’ sale; strongest discounts on Amazon’s own devices.

Was 6–7 Oct 2026

Prime Big Deal Days

Amazon’s October members’ event. It has finished for this year; many prices dip again for Black Friday.

Check a deal in 60 seconds

  1. Check the price history

    Use camelcamelcamel or Keepa (Amazon), or PriceRunner and idealo in the UK and Europe.

  2. Compare the exact model

    Sales often feature older or retailer-specific models with similar names. Match the model number.

  3. Check the seller

    On marketplaces, check who is actually selling and shipping it, and their return policy.

  4. Know your returns

    UK and EU online buyers usually get 14 days to cancel. US returns depend on the store’s policy.

Which gadgets get the biggest discounts?

Patterns repeat every year: Amazon’s own devices (Echo, Kindle, Fire TV, Ring) are discounted most heavily during Amazon events; robot vacuums, headphones, TVs and air fryers are Black Friday regulars; brand-new flagship phones and consoles rarely get big cash discounts, but bundles, gift cards and trade-in boosts are common.

Rules on “was” prices

In the EU, when a shop announces a price reduction it must show the lowest price it charged in the previous 30 days, so “was” prices can’t be inflated just before a sale. In the UK, the Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) can act against misleading pricing. In the US, the FTC has guidance on deceptive former-price comparisons. Even so, a price tracker is the quickest reality check.

Frequently asked questions

When is Black Friday 2026?
Black Friday 2026 is Friday 27 November, the day after US Thanksgiving (Thursday 26 November). Cyber Monday is 30 November 2026.
Is it better to buy tech on Black Friday or Cyber Monday?
There’s usually a lot of overlap: many deals run from Black Friday through Cyber Monday. Check price history on the item you want rather than waiting for a particular day.
